How It Works

1

Paste the article

Add the headline and the story body so the model sees more of the language, structure, and context around the claim.

2

Run the classifier

The app cleans the text, converts it into features, and generates a probability score for likely real or likely fake news.

3

Verify the result

Review the confidence score, source details, and the article itself before you treat the output as verified.

About & Verification Resources

Built for fast, careful checks

FakeNewsGuard is designed to help you screen suspicious articles quickly, but it does not replace editorial judgment or independent fact-checking.

Training dataset on Kaggle Live demo and deployment Source repository

Verification checklist

  • Check the original publisher and publication date.
  • Compare the claim against trusted outlets or primary documents.
  • Read beyond the headline and inspect the article body.
  • Treat low-confidence predictions as uncertain, not dismissed.
